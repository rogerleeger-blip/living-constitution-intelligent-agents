# Contributing and criticism

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/rogerleeger-blip/living-constitution-intelligent-agents/issues/new/choose) for objections, factual corrections and experiment proposals. One substantive question per issue helps discussion.

Please give the version and clause, explain the objection in its strongest form, separate factual from normative disagreement, cite relevant evidence, and suggest a counterexample, alternative or test. A solution is not required for a valid criticism.

Maintainers record each substantive objection in [the ledger](feedback/OBJECTIONS.md) with its source, date, status and response. Statuses: received; investigating; change proposed; addressed with linked revision; contested; deferred with reason. Reopening is welcome. Rejected suggestions retain a rationale and link; silence is not acceptance.

Disclose substantial AI assistance. Never present an AI-generated review as an independent person's judgment. Do not include credentials, private transcripts or identifying details of nonconsenting third parties. Describe security concerns without publishing exploitable secrets.

Proposed text contributions are included under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md) only with the contributor's agreement. Public discussion alone does not grant permission to reproduce a person's unrelated work. Attribute feedback and quote sparingly.

Useful first targets: behavioral measures of coercion; Sybil-resistant voting; explanation faithfulness; the distinction between persistence and legitimacy; simpler institutional baselines; evidence that would justify abandoning this seed.
