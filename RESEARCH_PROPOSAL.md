## Why it was retired from the active project

The Living Constitution is now treated primarily as an **open normative seed**, not as a rule set whose legitimacy should depend on a designed simulation.

The project is therefore focused on:

- public availability;
- open criticism;
- conceptual refinement;
- voluntary adoption or adaptation;
- agent-facing reflection rather than forced compliance;
- documenting real-world institutional cases that sharpen the principles;
- preserving provenance and objections.

Future researchers remain free to test the ideas empirically. Such work should not be interpreted as proving moral legitimacy merely because agents comply or a simulated society performs well.
