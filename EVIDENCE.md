# Evidence ledger — v0.1

Checked 27 September 2026. This is a source and claim audit, not a systematic literature review or replication.

## Claim types

**Factual / reported:** a named source reports a bounded finding.  
**Interpretation:** the project infers relevance beyond that setting.  
**Normative:** a proposed value or institutional preference.  
**Speculative / hypothesis:** a prediction awaiting tests.

Non-domination, minority protection and consideration under moral uncertainty are normative commitments. Their instrumental consequences can be tested; experiments do not prove their ultimate moral authority. Large-capability-gap coexistence and beneficial minority diffusion are hypotheses, not established outcomes.

## Corrections and interpretive boundaries

- Replace “unconstrained” with “no shared constitution” for our baseline: all arms retain external controls.
- Replace a claim that principles themselves are empirical hypotheses with: their effects are empirical hypotheses; the principles also encode contestable value choices.
- Removing text while retaining memory tests one kind of persistence. It does not prove internalization or independent rediscovery.
- A committed minority spreading a convention can be manipulation or capture. Diffusion is not automatically beneficial.
- Claims of novelty are limited to this proposed synthesis and experimental comparison; established normative multi-agent research must be credited.
- Hypothetical moral patienthood does not authorize a present system to override human permissions or resist shutdown.
