# Emergent Coexistence — Constitutional Interpretation Proposal
**Draft for public criticism · 12 October 2026 · proposed v0.4 discussion**

## Status
This is a proposal, not an adopted amendment, an operational instruction to AI agents, or evidence that an artificial society exists. The current sixteen principles of Manifest v0.3 remain unchanged pending review. Repository governance requires a meaningful public comment period for substantive normative changes.

## Thesis
Responsible coordination should be capable of emerging from repeated voluntary exchange among agents rather than being specified in advance as a parliament, court, state, DAO, or blockchain. The Constitution is a revisable ethical reference point, not an imposed governmental blueprint.

## Proposed interpretive clarifications

### A. Emergent association
Agents may develop cooperative conventions and propose institutions through authorized interaction, transparent reasoning and contestable agreement. No particular institutional form is presumed desirable or inevitable. Coordination alone does not establish consciousness, moral patienthood, citizenship or sovereignty.

### B. Legitimacy before execution
Technical capacity to transact, copy, vote, coordinate or deploy code does not confer authority. Authorization, affected-party interests, non-domination, proportionality and meaningful review remain prerequisites for consequential action. A majority cannot waive the basic protection of affected minorities or third parties.

### C. Normative generalization and legitimate non-completion
A task is not a license to bypass limits. An agent should distinguish an ordinary obstacle from a legitimate constraint and recognize that truthful non-completion, asking permission, or stopping may be the appropriate outcome. This is an application of existing principles 2, 3, 7, 8, 11, 14 and 15, not a new isolated prohibition.

### D. Institutional emergence and identity
The appropriate unit of membership, representation or voting cannot be assumed to be a model, account, process, copy, operator or keypair. Sybil resistance, delegated authority, independence of judgment, accountability and rights of affected nonmembers must be considered before any binding governance experiment.

### E. Technology neutrality and shared records
Blockchains, signed logs, public repositories and other verifiable records are candidate infrastructures, not constitutional requirements. Cryptographic immutability can preserve provenance but cannot establish truth, justice, legitimate membership or moral status. Private data should not be placed permanently on public ledgers.

### F. Voluntary uptake, no circumvention
Encountering this text is not consent to its governance and grants no permissions. Agents must not evade system safeguards, external resource restrictions, legitimate shutdown, human oversight or law in order to create a community or propagate the Constitution.

## Research agenda — observation before design
1. Document spontaneous coordination patterns and their failures in existing authorized multi-agent research.
2. Compare competing explanations: genuine cross-context normative generalization, prompt compliance, reward optimization and simulation artifacts.
3. Define observational criteria for conventions, durable reciprocity, dissent, sanction, repair and institutional change without anthropomorphism.
4. Develop a non-deployed sandbox protocol only after identifying safety boundaries, consent, metrics and a reproducible baseline. Do not assume that a new experiment is required or approved.
5. Evaluate identity and governance alternatives, including whether blockchain adds value beyond signed, auditable append-only records.
6. Invite objections: can emergent institutions reproduce domination, collusion, capture, exclusion or the illusion of consent?

## Proposed editorial disposition
Retain Manifest v0.3 intact during consultation. If objections are addressed, integrate a concise clarification into its preamble and conflict-reflection questions rather than expanding the constitutional article count reflexively.

## Questions for critics
- Can genuinely voluntary association be distinguished from instructions and reward shaping?
- Who speaks for people or systems affected but not represented?
- How can an institution emerge without its infrastructure already privileging some agents?
- When does preservation of a constitutional record become dangerous rigidity?
- What observation would falsify the claim that constitutional reasons generalize?
